A model with eyes, in WebAssembly

llama.cpp and its multimodal library run as a WebAssembly module. The program that drives them is Go, compiled by TinyGo, through the pkg/llamawasm package of yzma. This page decodes the image and sends the pixels to the program, which puts them in the prompt beside the question.

looking for a backend

On the CPU the image itself takes half a minute or more: the projector is the slow part, not the answer. With WebGPU it takes a second or two.
